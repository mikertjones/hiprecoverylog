Hip Replacement

A Personal Daily Recovery Log

A simple daily record of your recovery, for you and your care team. A free, printable log – made by a patient, after my own hip replacement.

A few lines a day show you how far you've come, help you spot patterns in pain, sleep and medicines, and give your physio, nurse or GP a clear picture at appointments.

What's inside

  • A Day 0 page for your operation, hospital stay and the names of your care team
  • 84 daily pages: medicines, activity sessions, pain, sleep, toilet visits, bowels, washing and walking aid
  • A weekly review after every seven days, so you can see your progress
  • Pages for your medicines, wound care, milestones and appointment questions

Why I made it

After my hip replacement I kept a daily record of everything: medicines, exercises, pain, sleep, and those night-time trips to the toilet. It helped me keep track of my progress, but it was one long electronic document. This log turns that record into clear daily pages that take a couple of minutes to fill in.

Many people are back to most everyday activities within 6 to 8 weeks. I hope that's true for you. But full recovery can take several months, so there are 12 weeks of daily pages: use as many as you need.

– Marty Jones
